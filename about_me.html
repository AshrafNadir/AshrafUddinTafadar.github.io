Hello! I'm Ashraf Uddin Tafadar, a data analyst with a strong foundation in Python, SQL, R, Power BI, Tableau, and Excel, alongside experience working with data, case information, and operational processes in a public-sector environment. My journey in data analysis is driven by a passion for uncovering meaningful insights from complex information, solving real-world problems, and transforming data into practical, evidence-based solutions.
I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Shahjalal University of Science & Technology and a Master's in Data Analytics from the University of Brighton. Throughout my academic and professional journey, I have developed strong analytical, technical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with a particular interest in using data to improve decision-making and create measurable value.
I currently work as a General Case Manager with the Office of the Public Guardian (OPG) within the Ministry of Justice, where I manage a varied caseload and analyse financial information, case documentation, and other complex information to support effective case management and decision-making. The role has strengthened my ability to work with sensitive and detailed information, identify key issues, prioritise competing demands, and communicate complex matters clearly to different audiences. Working within a public-sector environment has also given me valuable insight into how accurate information, structured analysis, and sound judgement can contribute to effective services and outcomes.
One of my proudest achievements was my Master's final project, where I worked with a renowned bus company to analyse and integrate data from three distinct datasets. I analysed 223,000+ records to investigate patterns in payment methods and passenger dwell time, using analytical techniques and tools to turn large volumes of raw data into meaningful findings. I worked closely with my academic supervisor and the company's stakeholder, who was non-technical, adapting my communication to present technical findings in a clear, accessible, and actionable way while meeting specific stakeholder requirements. I have also worked on several team-based projects throughout my Bachelor's and Master's degrees, developing my ability to collaborate, exchange ideas, solve problems collectively, and contribute effectively to shared objectives. These experiences, combined with my professional work, have helped me develop a practical approach to analytics that goes beyond technical implementation—I focus on understanding the problem, communicating the insight, and considering how analysis can lead to meaningful improvements.
I am particularly interested in the intersection of data, technology, and real-world decision-making. I enjoy turning seemingly disconnected numbers into coherent stories and using analytical thinking to identify opportunities, solve problems, and support better outcomes. My long-term ambition is to continue developing as a data professional, contribute to impactful analytical projects, share knowledge and ideas with the wider data community, and build work that demonstrates meaningful value beyond individual projects or organisations.
